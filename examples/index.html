Formats and inspection limits

Fabric JSON, Forge / NeoForge TOML, and Bedrock manifests, including nested packs. Limits: 4,096 entries, three nested levels, 256 KiB per metadata file, 16 MiB per nested package, 24 MiB inspected expansion, 160:1 compression ratio, and 128 metadata records. Split, encrypted and ZIP64 archives are unsupported.
